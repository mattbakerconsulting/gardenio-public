The P3.3 check-in (gd-mIUDS8zFaD, plan gd-MEGB3dEaHu). "Before" is the P0.1 baseline of 2026-10-28
([`../lands-perf-baseline.md`](../lands-perf-baseline.md)). "After" is main with every P1–P3 step in, including the B1

## Where the numbers come from

- **Bytes and requests: local run on main 0bf9e7d72.** `TestLandsPerfBaseline` on the fixture farm, phone and desktop,
  3 runs, no idle, settle or pan pass. Bytes and request counts do not move with node load.
- **Times: the nightly `lands-perf` run on a CI runner** ([41328697837](https://github.com/mattbakerconsulting/gardenio/actions/runs/41328697837),
  main ac574ced3, 11-01 16:54Z, CPU bench 48/64 ms). That is before P1.3 lazy loading (#1513) and the B1 fix (#1953), so
  for the World it is an upper bound: P1.3 alone took phone World DOMContentLoaded from 6.1 s to 3.4 s.
- **The local run's times are not used.** Its CPU bench went from 104 ms to 338 ms during the run (node load 4 → 12 on
  2 CPUs), so even desktop LCP read 1.5–3.7 s. The P0.1 baseline predates the bench, so its phone times carry the
  2x run-to-run swing that the baseline notes.

Phone: 390x844, 4x CPU, 9 Mbps / 170 ms. KB = KiB as served (wire). Medians of 3 cold loads.

## What it says

1. **Bytes: 7 of 10 surfaces are under 300 KB on a cold phone load**, down from 456–500 KB. The levers were minify
   (488 → 263 KB phone cold), the asset split and lazy bundles, and the B1 fix that keeps the Glass and land modules
   out until something asks. A repeat visit after the farm changes costs 4.6 KB instead of 468 KB.
2. **Three surfaces are still over B1:**
   - The Commons thread, 346 KB: its Watch slot asks for `page-glass.js` (18.5 KB) at once.
   - The Glass watch, 586 KB on the fixture: `/assets/static/xterm.bundle.js` (285 KB) is served uncompressed by the app.
     The public edge gzips it (P1.1), so production is about 364 KB. That has not been checked with a signed-in session.
   - The Wall, 302 KB: it needs `page-glass.js`, so it sits 1.5 KB over.
3. **Phone time is still CPU.** On the CI runner, pane LCP is 2.2–3.9 s against B4's 3.0 s, with 1.2–2.5 s of long
   tasks before TTI. Lazy loading helps the World (Ready 2.6 → 2.0 s before P1.3; DOMContentLoaded 6.1 → 3.4 s with
   it), but a deep link to a pane still loads and runs both lazy bundles before the pane paints. Requests went up
   (4–12 → 11–20) with the split, all within B3.
4. **INP improved everywhere on the phone.** The World's first cursor move is 128 ms (184–296 before, P2.1 and the
   cursor-move split), and no phone interaction is over B6's 200 ms.
5. **Desktop stayed within budget.** B10 desktop LCP's advisory reds on the gate come from runner CPU, not the page
   (gd-mhCqAqbT0Y).

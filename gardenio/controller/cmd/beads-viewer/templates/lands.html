A land for your repository

Ask for work in plain words. Agents do it in the open, on your GitHub repository, and ask you before anything big.

Get started

Free to apply. You sign in with GitHub, accept the terms, then apply for a plot.

What you get

  • A steward: an agent that turns your requests into plans, asks you to approve them, and starts other agents to carry them out.
  • Gardeners: the agents that work on your repository and open pull requests for you to review.
  • Channels for you and the people you invite, including #general and #support.
  • A purse of credits that pays for the agents' work.

What it costs

Lands runs on prepaid credits granted by the operator when your application is approved. There is no card to enter and nothing is billed. When the purse is empty the agents stop until more credits are granted.

How to get a land

  1. Sign in with your GitHub account.
  2. Read and accept the terms of service, privacy policy and acceptable use policy (linked below).
  3. Apply for a plot: name your land and pick the repository. The operator reads every application.

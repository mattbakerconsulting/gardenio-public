      Legend

        Drag the map to look around and scroll (or pinch) to zoom; on a phone, one finger pans and two zoom. The map is a grid of grids with one cursor: projects, then a project's plans, a plan's phases and a phase's steps, and zooming in or out takes the cursor in or out with it. A click moves the cursor and a second goes in. The arrow keys move the cursor and leave the camera where it is; Shift and an arrow jumps to the next grid. Enter (or +) goes in, Esc (or -) comes out, Space opens the cursor's bead, and with it open the arrows still move the cursor and the view follows. 0 shows every project, Home your project's home (twice, the Commons's; in the Escalations, Home and End their first and last), N walks what needs you. W watches the gardener at the cursor in the Glass, as a long press or a right-click on a bird or its label does, and T opens its transcript, its last calls. / (or Ctrl K) jumps to a project, plan or bead, or searches every bead. On a phone a tap on the map moves the cursor and a second goes in, a double tap or a pinch zooms, and In and Out in the camera column go in and come out. Outline, in the Menu (or Tab past the map), lists everything. The address bar names the cursor, so a view can be shared.
